Jab:
Stage 1: Fighter preparing, if hit stop jab
Stage 2: Throw jab, hit collision
Stage 3: Recovering
During these stages the fighter can not move neither Block.
By combining a Jab with Crouch, throws a low punch.
By combining a Jab with a Directional Move, throws a punch with the same direction boost.

Block:
Stage 1: Fighter preparing, if hit stop jab
Stage 2: Block
Stage 3: Recovering
During these stages the fighter can not throw a Jab.
By combining a Block with Crouch, defends a low punch.
