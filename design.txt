a 2d pixel art style, with nice colors
in 3:2 aspect ratio

a wooden bar.
the counter comes from the right edge and curves to the wall around the middle of the screen,
behind the counter, close to the right edge of the screen a door.
on the left a few tables and chairs, along with the entrance.

character 1: a bald white guy, with a white top and jeans
character 2: (not sure yet)

the two characters are sat next to eachother in high chairs, you see them from behind.
they are arguing loudly, and in a second they start cursing about eachother moms.
character 1 stands up, super mad, and starts walking away, when the other character grabs his beer bottle and throws it at him.
character 1 turns around super angry, while character 2 stands up from his chair, and character 1 punches him in the face.
now they are both standing up, and fighting, round 1 begins.
the bar has a few people around watching, and the bartender is instigating them.
until, character 2 gets knocked out.

round 2 starts, both back on their feet, and bit more tired and breathless.
a few more people appeared in the bar.
hands start appearing on the edge waving money at the characters.
this time character 1 gets knocked out.

round 3 starts, both back on their feet, and even more tired and breathless.
a big camera and a commentator behind the counter are now watching the fight.
a big crowd, now in the bar watching.

one of them finnaly drops, and the game is over.

from an opening door behind the counter, comes out the manager shouting at the bartender:
- What the hell is happening here?
and then to the crowd:
- All of you get the fuck out!
the screen fades to black
